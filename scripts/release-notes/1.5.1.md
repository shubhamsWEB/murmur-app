## What's new on Windows

**Murmur Cleanup on Windows.** AI cleanup can now use Murmur's own cleanup model on your PC, as it does on the Mac: it tidies dictation in a fraction of a second and keeps your words. Turn on **AI clean-up** in **Settings → Commands & AI**, then choose **Download** next to **Clean-up model** (0.5 GB, through Ollama). Until it's downloaded, or with **Same as commands** chosen, clean-up uses the commands' model as before.

**Stop a command.** Press Esc, or choose **Stop Command** in the tray, and the command stops after what it's doing at that moment. A new command now stops the one before it, so two never run at once.

**Answer Murmur's questions.** When Murmur asks something partway through ("Which file do you mean?"), just say the answer and the same task carries on.

## Fixes

- Windows: a command no longer stops with "expected element type" when the local model writes a tool call Ollama can't read. Murmur asks the model again.
- Windows: AI clean-up no longer runs out of time while its model loads: it loads while you speak.

On the Mac, nothing changes in this version.
