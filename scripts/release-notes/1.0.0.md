## What's new

**Speak Hinglish.** Murmur now hears three ways of speaking, and you pick yours when you set it up (or later in Settings): **English**, **Hinglish** — talk in Hindi and English mixed, and it's typed in English letters ("kal meeting 5 baje hai") — and **Other languages**. Each model downloads only after you choose it, and the listening pill shows its progress.

**Commands in Hinglish.** "Mujhe PDF kholkar do", "Mama ko WhatsApp pe bolo ki main late hoon", "Figma kholo" run just like their English versions.

**Big jobs, done all the way.** Ask for something with many parts, like "go through my unread emails and tell me what's important", and Murmur writes itself a task list, keeps notes as it goes, goes back when it needs to, and doesn't stop until every item is done.

**Permissions like Claude's.** When Murmur needs your OK it asks once: **Allow**, **Allow for This Task** (it then works on its own until the task is finished), or **Always Allow** that kind of action. Settings → Permissions shows what's always allowed and takes it back. Anything that pays or transfers money always asks.

**Skills.** Teach the agent how you want things done with skills, in the same SKILL.md format Claude uses. Murmur comes with its own skill for working carefully on your Mac (look, act, check it worked) and the Cua Driver skill for on-screen control, which turns on by itself once Cua Driver is installed. Add any skill from a GitHub link in Settings → Skills, or drop your own in the Skills folder.

**Your keys, your way.** Hold **fn** and Murmur works out whether you're writing or asking. Add **⇧** to only type, or **⌃** to only run a command. Every shortcut can be changed in Settings → Shortcuts: the talk key (fn, Right ⌥ or Right ⌘), the two modifiers, and file search (⇧⌘Space).

**More skills, longer jobs.** Murmur now comes with skills for going through email, catching up on chats, researching on the web, comparing prices while shopping, filling in forms, and working through long tasks item by item. If a big job runs out of time, say **"continue"** and it picks up where it stopped.

**Summaries of the whole page.** "Summarise this" reads all of a web page, not just what's on screen, and questions about your own chats, email and calendar ("what did I send Arjun on WhatsApp today?") are answered instead of typed.

**One Settings page.** Speech, shortcuts, commands, permissions, skills, indexing and recordings are all in one place.

## Better at the everyday things

- Opening a document opens the one you meant: the search's best matches go to the AI, which picks by what you said, in English or Hinglish.
- WhatsApp messages are recognised however you ask, sent once, and checked that they really went.
- The listening pill appears the moment you press the key.
- Without Ollama installed, a command that needs a model says so and opens the place to set one up.

## Fixes

- Opening your documents: "open my PAN card" or "aadhaar card kholo" finds the file by its name (PanCard.pdf, Aadhar.pdf), and "my driving license and PAN card" opens both.
- First launch downloads nothing until you choose: setup says what it downloads (the file search model, then the speech model for the language you pick), one at a time, so each is fast.

- Setup asks you to try dictation only once the speech model is ready.
- The Hinglish model no longer gets stuck on "Preparing".
