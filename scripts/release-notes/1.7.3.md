## What's new on the Mac

**Your day, in full.** Clicking the morning brief or the evening wrap-up now opens a page with everything behind it: what needs you (due and overdue, replies you owe, people to chase), today's plans, your reminders, where you left off, and what you worked on, session by session, with what got done. A night's work reads as one session, not two days. You can also open it from Memory.

**Notifications open where you expect.** Clicking one no longer closes Murmur's window when another copy of Murmur is installed.
