## What's new

**Murmur for Windows.** The Windows app is out of beta and catches up with the Mac: dictation in any app (English, Hinglish and 25 European languages), AI clean-up, voice commands and file search, in the same sidebar window. It installs for your account with no admin rights and now updates itself.

**Voice commands on Windows.** Hold **Ctrl + Windows** and say "open Spotify", "volume 30", "search YouTube for lofi" or "summarise this", and Murmur does it instead of typing it. Add **Alt** to make anything you say a command, or **Shift** to have it typed exactly as you said it, as **fn + ⌃** and **fn + ⇧** do on the Mac. Commands open apps, Settings pages, folders and websites, change the volume and brightness, control media, write in an app, open your files by description, and rewrite, translate or summarise selected text. They also run PowerShell commands ("how much space is left on C"), asking you first before anything that changes something. They use Ollama on your PC, or your Claude or OpenAI key, set in **Settings → Commands & AI**; common ones need no model at all.

**AI clean-up on Windows.** Turn it on in **Settings → Commands & AI** to remove "um"s and false starts, apply your self-corrections ("at 5, no, 6pm" → "at 6pm") and set out spoken lists, in the style of the app you're in. **Paste Exactly What I Said** in the tray types the words as they were heard.

## Fixes

- Windows: the dictation key keeps working when Murmur has been open for hours. Windows could quietly stop passing the key on; Murmur now notices, picks it up again, and still counts that press.
- Windows: the speech and search models download on PCs without Visual Studio's runtime. The installer was missing `vcomp140.dll` ("VCOMP140.DLL was not found").
- Windows: a problem loading the Hinglish model no longer closes Murmur. Murmur says what went wrong and English dictation keeps working. On some PCs Hinglish doesn't load yet.
