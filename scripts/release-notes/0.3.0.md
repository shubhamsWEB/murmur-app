## What's new

**Murmur remembers.** Tell it once and it uses it everywhere: “remember that Mom is Roma on WhatsApp”, “from now on open links in Chrome”, “call me Shubham”. It also notices your habits (who you message where, the sites you keep opening) and follows on from your last command: after “send a message to Roma saying hi”, just say “and also ask when she's coming back”. Everything it knows is on the new **Memory** page, each line with a × to forget it. It stays on your Mac.

**It gets the whole job done.** Tasks check they're actually finished before stopping, keep to one tab, read long pages all the way down, and say what they found if they run out of steps. A slow model no longer cuts a task short.

**It works where you are.** Murmur acts on the app and window you're looking at: “play this video” in Finder or QuickTime plays that video, “open the second one” opens the second file in your Finder search, and “this” means what's on your screen.

**Your apps, not just your browser.** “What's on my calendar tomorrow?” and “what are my reminders?” read your calendars and reminders directly. Mail, Notes, Music, Contacts and any other scriptable app can be read too.

**Messages.** “Send a message to Roma on WhatsApp saying I'll be late”, “text Mom that…”, “tell the team on Slack…” are sent for you (WhatsApp and Messages to the number in Contacts), always after you see the exact words and say yes.

**Copy and paste across apps.** “Copy the text from the other page and paste it here” and “paste what I copied” put the text back in the box you were typing in.

**Your documents.** “Open my Aadhaar card” and “show my resume” look for your own files by your name, and “open the pdf” no longer opens the same document every time.

## Better dictation

- Normal and fast speech are heard as well as loud, close speech: quiet words are lifted, and the first and last words are no longer cut off.
- Whisper Mode is now **Quiet Voice**. It boosts soft speech; like all dictation, it runs on your Mac.

## Fixes

- Permission questions (Calendar, controlling an app, Screen Recording) now appear in front instead of behind your windows.
- Dictation that starts with “Also…” or “And…” is typed, not run as a command.
- Play and pause act on the player in front instead of an older video in another window.
