## What's new

**Murmur's own models, on your Mac.** Two small models trained for Murmur, downloaded through Ollama and run entirely on your Mac:
- **Murmur Agent** carries out commands. Steps take a couple of seconds where the general model took half a minute, and it picks the right tool more often. Choose it in **Commands & AI → Local model**; Murmur downloads it (2.8 GB). If you already use a local model, it stays as it is until you switch.
- **Murmur Cleanup** tidies dictation in a fraction of a second and keeps your words: fillers out, self-corrections applied, lists set out. Turn on **AI cleanup** in Settings and download it there (0.5 GB). From then on it's used for cleanup, or you can choose your commands' model instead.

**Stop a command.** Press Esc, or choose **Stop Command** in the menu bar, and the command stops after what it's doing at that moment. If it had a to-do list, say "continue" to pick it up. A new command now stops the one before it, so two never run at once.

**Answer Murmur's questions.** When Murmur asks something partway through ("Which resume do you mean?"), just say the answer ("the latest one") and the same task carries on.

**Messages to anyone in your chats.** Murmur opens a WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Discord or Teams chat by the person's name, even when they have no phone number in Contacts, then sends your message or file.

**Quicker screen vision.** Murmur reads the text on screen with the Mac's own text recognition and asks the vision model only what the picture shows, so looking at an app takes seconds, not a minute. The default vision model is now Qwen3-VL 4B Instruct (3.3 GB). Until you download it, Murmur keeps using the one you have.

## Fixes

- Murmur no longer asks for Screen Recording at every launch when it already has it.
- "Message hi to Roma on WhatsApp" goes to Roma, not to someone called "Hi To Roma".
- Murmur doesn't try AppleScript on apps that can't be scripted (WhatsApp, Slack and most chat apps), which used to send a command round in circles.
- A command no longer stops with "expected element type" when the local model writes a tool call Ollama can't read. Murmur asks the model again.
- AI cleanup no longer runs out of time while its model loads.
- "Send my resume to Roma" sends it, instead of stopping at the search results.
