## What's new

**AI cleanup.** Turn on *Tidy up what you say* in Settings to clean up dictations automatically: it removes "um", "uh", and repeated words, applies self-corrections ("at 5, no, 6pm" → "at 6pm"), and formats spoken lists cleanly for each app. If you ever want the unedited words back, the menu bar's *Paste Exactly What I Said* restores the verbatim transcript.

**Choose your microphone.** Settings → Speech now lets you choose which microphone Murmur listens with. You can also tell it to prefer the Mac's built-in microphone over AirPods, avoiding the low-bandwidth phone-call audio mode Bluetooth headsets switch into.

**Murmur button on screen.** Turn on the on-screen button for a draggable Murmur pill on your desktop. Click it to open Murmur, right-click to dictate or hide it, and place it anywhere you like.

**Rich answers and task traces.** The floating answer window renders full Markdown with headings, checklists, code blocks, and tables, with one-click copying to clean plain text. An expandable trace shows the skills used and every step taken during a task.

## Fixes

- Screen control recognizes when a click doesn't change the view and tries alternative paths (like direct URLs or shortcuts) instead of getting stuck or asking you to click.
- Task health tracking detects repeating actions and redirects the agent before it runs out of steps.
