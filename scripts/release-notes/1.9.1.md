## Fixes on the Mac

**Dictation is typed, not run as a command, when it's meant for someone else.** What you dictate into Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or another AI app (in its app or in a browser) is typed into its box, however much it sounds like an instruction. So is anything over 25 words in any text box. Before, a long note starting "Check if…", or "update the website and release it" said into Claude, could be run as a command for Murmur and never typed. To give Murmur a command there, start with "Murmur, …" or hold ⌃ with fn. Short ones like "open Slack" still work as before.

**Sarvam no longer swaps your words for the window's.** Murmur used to send Sarvam the words in the front window's title to listen for, and Sarvam sometimes wrote them over what you said: "Murmur ki website" said into Claude came back "Claude ki website". Now Sarvam gets only the words you list in Settings.

**Long dictations keep every word.** A dictation over 30 seconds goes to Sarvam in pieces. They're now cut at a real pause, and each piece gets a moment of silence at both ends, so the word at a cut isn't lost.

**Numbers in digits.** With AI cleanup on, numbers you say are written the way people write them: "version one point three" → "version 1.3", "P two P one" → "P2 P1", "twenty five people" → "25 people", "ten percent" → "10%", "fifty dollars" → "$50", "at eleven thirty" → "at 11:30". One to nine in an ordinary sentence ("I have two kids") stay as words.

**Cleanup never drops what you said.** AI cleanup may take out fillers, words said twice and the phrase you corrected, and nothing else. If it leaves out anything more, your words go in exactly as you said them.

**Your side of the meeting stays in the transcript.** A reply that repeated the other person's words ("Can you share the pricing sheet?" / "Yes, I'll share the pricing sheet") was taken for the call echoing through your speakers and left out. Now only the same words, in the same order, at the same moment count as an echo.

**Who's speaking.** Meeting notes now tell the voices on a call apart, on your Mac: one other person is "Them", and with more they're "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2" and so on, in the transcript, the answers, the notes and the saved file. The voice model is downloaded once (about 30 MB).
