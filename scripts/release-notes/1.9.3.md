## The Murmur button, on Mac and Windows

**It opens when you point at it.** Rest the pointer on the small Murmur button and it opens into three icons: the starling opens Murmur, the microphone starts dictation, and the note takes meeting notes. A small label names the one you're on. While notes are being taken the note becomes a stop square, and the small button shows a coral dot.

**The listening pill opens smoothly.** Its words no longer change while it's still opening. "Starting the microphone…" only shows when the microphone is slow to come on.

## On the Mac

**Drag Murmur to Applications.** The download now opens as a window with Murmur and your Applications folder side by side.

## Windows catches up

**A tour of Murmur.** The first time you open Murmur after this update, a starling shows you around the window: talking, commands, file search, meeting notes and the settings worth knowing. It's in About & Help whenever you want it again.

**Meetings: who said what.** Murmur now tells the voices on a call apart on your PC and labels them Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on. A line is cut where the voice changes, so one person's words don't run on under another's label. Click a speaker in the transcript to name them, and choose "Remember this voice" to have Murmur know them in later meetings (Meetings › People). A voice is named from the conversation only when that person says their own name. The voice models are downloaded once (32 MB).

**The call is no longer written down as "You".** On speakers, your microphone hears the call too. Those lines are left out, and your own words are kept when you speak over the speakers.

**Smaller meeting fixes.** Lines stay in the order they were said. Final notes use the speakers' labels and have room for long meetings. A meeting with nobody speaking ends itself after ten minutes (Meetings › End by itself).

**Dictation is typed, not run as a command, when it's meant for someone else.** What you dictate into Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or another AI app, or at length into any text box, is typed. Saying "Murmur, …" or holding Alt still makes it a command.

**Long dictations keep every word.** A dictation over 30 seconds goes to Sarvam in pieces. They're now cut at a real pause, with a moment of silence at each edge.

**Numbers in digits.** With AI clean-up on, numbers you say are written the way people write them: "version one point three" → "version 1.3".

**Clean-up never drops what you said.** It may take out fillers, words said twice and the phrase you corrected, and nothing else. If it leaves out anything more, your words go in as you said them.
