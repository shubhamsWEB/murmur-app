## What's new

**Installs itself in Applications.** Open Murmur from the downloaded disk image (or from Downloads) and it offers to move itself to your Applications folder, opens again from there, and ejects the disk image. No more dragging it onto the Applications shortcut. If an older Murmur is already there, it's replaced; if macOS needs an administrator's permission, it asks for your password.

**Murmur's icon on its disk image.** The disk image shows Murmur's icon on your desktop and in Finder while it's open.
