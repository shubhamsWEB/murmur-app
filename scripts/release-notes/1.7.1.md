## What's new on the Mac

**Questions about your own work are answered from memory.** "What did we release, and have we posted it on LinkedIn and WhatsApp?" is answered from what Murmur saw you do, instead of searching the web for it. A question with two parts gets both answered; if Murmur has seen one part and not the other, it says which, and offers to check. It no longer finishes a question with nothing to say.

**One memory, searched as one.** What you did, what you sent and posted, your conversations, what you promised and what Murmur learned about your world now sit in a single index, searched at once by words, by meaning, by the people and places named, by the time asked about and by what's still owed. "The release" finds "Releasing Murmur 1.7.0", "yesterday" finds yesterday, and "what do I need to do?" finds your open promises. Every task, whichever model plans it, local or cloud, starts with what memory knows about it.

**Hears names the way you say them.** When speech to text mishears a name, Murmur reads it as the name your memory knows: "Thanai" and "Tana" are Tanay, "Herschel" is Harshal. Versions said aloud become numbers ("one point seven" is 1.7). Real words are left alone.

**Remembers what you post and send.** Your LinkedIn posts, WhatsApp messages (groups included) and release tags pushed from Terminal or GitHub are kept as things you did. After the update, Murmur reads the windows it already kept once more, so posts from before it are found too.
