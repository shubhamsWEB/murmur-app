## What's new on the Mac

**Commands finish the job.** Asked to fill something in, Murmur now enters it rather than working out the answer and stopping, saying what it will do next, or asking whether to go on. It acts on what it has read rather than looking at the screen again and again, and if it truly can't finish, it says what stopped it. A follow-up in the same window builds on what the last command did.

**Types where pasting doesn't work.** Grids, games and other places that only listen for key presses now get real key presses, and Murmur no longer reports typing that never reached the screen. Pages read with their rows, columns and switch states, so "row 3, column 5" and "turn it off" mean what they say.

**Hears your first word.** The microphone stays ready for a minute after you dictate, so the next dictation starts at once and includes the half second before you pressed the key. A word you start as the key goes down now arrives whole. Turn it off in Settings under **Catch your first word**.

**Clearer dictation.** Clear voice, built for calls rather than transcription, is now off unless you turn it on for a noisy room. Murmur changes your voice less before the speech model hears it, and your last word is no longer dropped. If your microphone sounds quiet, raise its input level in System Settings › Sound.

**Spells names right.** Murmur listens for the names in the window you're dictating into, and for words you add under **Your words** in Settings. It only corrects a word it wasn't sure it heard, and never one it heard clearly.
