## What's new on the Mac and Windows

**Fix grammar.** Turn on **Fix grammar** in Settings and Murmur corrects grammar mistakes in what you dictate, and nothing else: "he go" becomes "he goes", "I going" becomes "I am going", "two file" becomes "two files". Your words, their order and their meaning stay as you said them, and Hinglish is left alone. If the fix would change anything more than grammar, or takes longer than 4 seconds, your words go in exactly as you said them. It runs on the same small model as AI cleanup, so with both turned on there's no extra wait. It's off unless you turn it on.
