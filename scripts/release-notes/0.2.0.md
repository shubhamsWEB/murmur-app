## What's new

**Murmur works inside your browser.** In Chrome, Safari, Brave, Edge and Arc it reads the page you're on, clicks links and buttons by their words, fills in and submits fields, scrolls, switches tabs and pauses the video. “Open saleshq.ai and summarise it” now reads the whole page. Turn on **Allow JavaScript from Apple Events** once (Chrome: View → Developer; Safari: Develop menu).

**Murmur works in your apps.** It can script Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Mail, Messages, Finder, Music and macOS settings directly: “remind me to call Mom at 6”, “what's on my calendar tomorrow”, “draft an email to Rahul”, “turn on dark mode”. Anything that sends, deletes or quits asks first.

**It sees your screen.** “What's on my screen?” works in any app, using the window's text, or a picture of it when you allow Screen Recording.

**More Mac switches:** Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on or off, brightness to a percentage, and pause, resume or skip whatever is playing.

**Coding follow-ups.** After Murmur starts Claude Code or Codex on a project, “add a login page” or “run it” goes to that same session in Terminal.

**Longer tasks finish.** Requests with several parts no longer stop after opening the first page, and questions about the screen or your Mac are recognised as commands in any app.

## Fixes

- Murmur no longer crashes when it asks you to confirm something (quitting an app, running a command).
- The pill shows a spinner while working and a check when done; the warning icon only appears for real problems.
