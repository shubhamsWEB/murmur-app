## Meetings on the Mac: who said what

**Each person on a call gets their own lines.** Murmur now tells the voices on a call apart and labels them Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on. When someone starts talking the moment another person stops, the line is cut where the voice changes, so one person's words no longer run on under another's label.

**Name a voice once and Murmur knows it next time.** Click a speaker in the meeting panel to name them, and choose "Remember this voice" to keep it. In later meetings that person is named by voice alone, or Murmur asks when it isn't sure. Saved voices stay on this Mac; manage them under Meetings › People.

**Names are only applied when they're certain.** A voice is named from the conversation only when that person says their own name ("Hi, I'm Priya"). Other names heard in the meeting are offered as one-click choices when you name someone. Before, a name spoken near a voice could be put on the wrong person.

**The call is no longer written down as "You".** On speakers, your microphone hears the call too. Those lines are now left out, and your own words are kept when you speak over the speakers.

**Smaller fixes.** Lines stay in the order they were said. No words are lost where a line is cut between two speakers (with the on-Mac English model). Final notes use the speakers' current labels, don't invent a name or gender for anyone, and have room for long meetings. A meeting with nobody speaking ends itself after ten minutes (Meetings › End by itself).

## Tap to write on the Mac

**It knows which messages are yours.** In a chat, Murmur now marks your messages and the other person's before asking the AI, so a reply is written from your side.

**It reads apps that say little about themselves.** When a window gives Murmur next to nothing to read (WhatsApp is one), it asks again more patiently, reads the descriptions the app gives for its messages, and as a last step reads the conversation from a picture of the window. The picture is read on this Mac and isn't kept.

**It uses what Murmur remembers.** The reply now draws on your profile, on what memory holds about the person you're writing to, and on what it holds about the conversation.

**It learns how you write.** Your own messages in the thread, messages you sent before in that app, and what you changed in Murmur's text are shown to the AI as examples. This starts empty and improves as you use it; it's kept in a file on this Mac.

**It checks itself.** A time, date, amount or link that isn't in anything the AI was given is caught, and the reply is written again without it.
